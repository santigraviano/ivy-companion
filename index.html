Ivy Companion

Ivy is a personal voice and text assistant that runs on its developer's own Mac. It is a private, single-user project: it is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What Ivy does with Google

With permission, Ivy reads its owner's Google Calendar (read-only, scope calendar.readonly) so it can answer questions like "what's on tomorrow?" and mention upcoming events. Ivy never creates, changes or deletes Google Calendar events.

See the privacy policy for how that data is handled.
