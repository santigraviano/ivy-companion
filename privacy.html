Privacy policy

Ivy Companion is a private app with a single user, its owner.

It requests read-only access to Google Calendar and uses that data only to answer the owner's questions about their schedule. The sign-in token stays on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Ivy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
